# Data in `sky.bin`

`sky.bin` is generated by `tool/build_catalog.dart` (format: see
`packages/astro/lib/src/catalog/codec.dart`). It contains data derived from:

1. **Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th Revised Ed.** (Hoffleit D., Warren Jr W.H.,
   Astronomical Data Center, NSSDC/ADC, 1991; CDS catalogue V/50), as
   distributed by the Harvard-Smithsonian Center for Astrophysics
   (http://tdc-www.harvard.edu/catalogs/bsc5.html). Positions, magnitudes,
   B−V colours, spectral types, proper motions, parallaxes and designations of
   9096 stars are kept at catalogue precision; the 14 removed objects are
   skipped.
2. **ConstellationLines** by Marc van der Sluys (https://hemel.waarnemen.com,
   https://github.com/MarcvdSluys/ConstellationLines, commit 158ea7c),
   licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
   (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: the lists of BSC
   numbers were converted to the binary polyline table; no lines were added,
   removed or reordered.
3. `data/constellations.txt` and `data/star_names.txt` in this repository:
   IAU constellation names and common star names (spellings of the IAU Working
   Group on Star Names), compiled for this project.
